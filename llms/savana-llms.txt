# Savana

> Savana is a Malvern, Pennsylvania financial software company (founded 2009) selling a cloud-native,
> API-first Digital Delivery Platform to banks, credit unions and fintechs. It sits above the core
> banking system rather than replacing it, unifying banker-facing servicing (CRM, BPM, assisted
> account opening) and customer-facing digital banking on one workflow foundation.

Savana markets "Banking APIs" that orchestrate whole servicing processes between assisted and
self-service channels. As of 2026-08-05 those APIs are **not publicly documented**: there is no
developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL specification, no SDK, no sandbox,
and no public status page. Product documentation and the status page are behind a customer login
(HappyFox) and Microsoft Entra ID SSO respectively. Access appears to be sold through the
"Request a Demo" sales path.
